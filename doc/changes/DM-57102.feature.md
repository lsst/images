Added a `multithreaded` option to `read_archive`, `open_archive` and the `read` methods of the image classes.
When it is set, the image, mask and variance of a `MaskedImage`, `VisitImage` or `CellCoadd` in a FITS file are read and decompressed in separate threads, provided they are compressed in tiles of at least 8000 pixels.
Files in smaller tiles, where threads are slower, are still read in the calling thread, as is everything by default.
Reads through the butler formatter are multithreaded.
